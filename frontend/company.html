About HSMLE India Corporation

Founded in 2019, HSMLE India Corporation has established itself as an authoritative manufacturer and engineering supplier of state-of-the-art testing machines and precision inspection equipment.

Our core expertise encompasses Universal Testing Machines (UTM), Hardness Testers (Rockwell, Brinell, Vickers, Micro-Vickers), Metallographic Specimen Preparation Equipment (Abrasive Cutters, Mounting Presses, Auto Polishers), Optical Metrology Systems (Video Measuring Systems, Profile Projectors, Metallurgical Microscopes), and Environmental Test Chambers.

Our solutions are deployed across automotive component manufacturing, aerospace precision workshops, railway wagon & coach factories, defense armaments, steel foundries, plastic polymer testing, and technical universities.
